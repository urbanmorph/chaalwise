# Terms

These terms cover [Chaalwise](https://chaalwise.urbanmorph.com), a free learning tool made by [UrbanMorph](https://www.urbanmorph.com/). By using it, you agree to them. Last changed on 20 October 2026; every change is in this file's history.

## A learning tool, not a gaming service

Chaalwise teaches thinking skills: thinking ahead, weighing odds, sizing a risk, keeping a cool head, and more, for work, study and everyday life. Games are its teaching material. A chess position, a poker hand or a short game-theory exercise shows an idea, and the learner practises it in a few questions. Learners do not play full games here, against anyone; Chaalwise points them elsewhere for that. It is free, works offline once loaded, and needs no account.

## Never a money game

Chaalwise is not offered as a game. Still, because it uses games to teach, here is how it stands under the [Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/8a7f103cefc68ed8aaa2ebc9a2ed7c13.pdf) (the Act) and the [Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/271974.pdf) (the Rules), G.S.R. 303(E), in force from 1 May 2026.

- **Nothing in Chaalwise is an online money game.** The Act defines one as a game "played by a user by paying fees, depositing money or other stakes in expectation of winning which entails monetary and other enrichment" (section 2(1)(g)). Chaalwise has no fees, deposits, stakes or winnings of any kind.
- **Practice points are not "other stakes".** The Act's "other stakes" are things "equivalent or convertible to money", including virtual credits, coins or tokens, "purchased by paying money directly or by indirect means" (section 2(1)(j)). The points used in poker practice and the labs are free and unlimited. They can never be bought, sold, transferred, cashed out or exchanged for prizes, and they have no value outside Chaalwise.
- **Any part that could be called a game is an online social game.** The Act's online social game "does not involve staking of money or other stakes" and is offered "solely for entertainment, recreation or skill-development purposes" (section 2(1)(i)). Chaalwise is offered for skill development.
- **Against each factor the Authority weighs** in deciding whether a game is a money game (rule 9): no fees, deposits or stakes at any stage; no expectation of winning money or anything else; no revenue model, as Chaalwise charges nothing and carries no ads; and nothing that can be "transferred, redeemed, monetised or used outside the game environment".
- **No real-money game is promoted.** Chaalwise carries no advertising, links or referrals for any real-money game, card or betting site, directly or indirectly (section 6 of the Act).
- **Not determined or registered, and not claiming to be.** Chaalwise has not been determined or registered by the Online Gaming Authority of India, and does not say it has (rule 15(b)). Registration is required only where the Central Government notifies a game or category (rule 12(1)(a)); if it ever does so for something like Chaalwise, we will register.

If you go on to play poker away from Chaalwise, play only at home for points or counters that are worth nothing, never for money.

## Your progress stays with you

- What you do in Chaalwise is stored in your own browser, on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- There are no accounts, ads, tracking cookies or third-party trackers.
- You can export your progress to a file and import it on another device from the progress page.
- If you clear your browser's storage, your progress is deleted, and we cannot recover it: we never had it.

## Questions, problems and complaints

[Raise an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/urbanmorph/chaalwise/issues/new). Say what happened, on which page, and on which device and browser if it matters. Issues are public, so do not include personal details. We aim to reply within 15 days.

Where any part of Chaalwise is treated as an online social game, this is its grievance redressal mechanism under rule 20 of the Rules. If you are not satisfied with our reply, or get no reply, you can appeal to the Online Gaming Authority of India within 30 days of our decision (rule 20(2)), and from there to the Appellate Authority (rule 7).

A straight is five ranks in a row, such as five, six, seven, eight and nine, of any suits; the ace counts high or low. A full house is three cards of one rank and two of another. Four of a kind is four cards of one rank, and three of a kind is three. Two pair is two different pairs. High card means none of these, so your highest card counts. When two players have the same kind of hand, the higher cards that make it win: a pair of kings beats a pair of queens. If those are equal, compare the other cards from the top down.'

---
title: The group project
summary: Four students, six weeks and one report. Plan the work, deal with a missed deadline, choose what to cut, and look back calmly.
game: case
stage: 1
order: 1
skills: [plan-long, cooperate, composure]
steps:
  - kind: read
    id: intro
    text: 'You and three classmates have six weeks to write a group report on how people in your town get to work. It is worth 30% of the course mark. There are four parts: background reading, a survey, the analysis and the writing.'
  - ask: cases/group-project/plan-the-weeks
  - kind: read
    id: missed
    text: You agree a weekly plan. Arjun takes the survey questions, due on the Monday of week three. By Thursday nothing has come, and he has not answered the group chat. The survey cannot go out without his questions.
  - ask: cases/group-project/missed-deadline
  - kind: read
    id: take-stock
    text: 'Arjun had been ill. He sends the questions two days later. The survey gets 50 replies, not the 120 you hoped for. In week five you take stock. The four of you have 6 hours each left before the hand-in, 24 hours in all. The work left is 30 hours: 10 for the analysis, 12 for the writing, and 8 for an extra chapter comparing two towns.'
  - ask: cases/group-project/what-to-cut
  - kind: read
    id: the-mark
    text: You hand in on time, and the report gets a B. A friend's group, who did everything in the last week, got an A. Someone in your group says all the planning was a waste of time.
  - ask: cases/group-project/look-back
  - kind: plan
    id: plan
    prompt: Use it this week. When will you split work into steps you can check?
    situations:
      - I start a group project or an assignment
      - a deadline is more than two weeks away
      - a big piece of work feels too large to start
    action: I will agree one finished piece for each week, and check it with the others
---

A story in four decisions, for students. It uses [A plan, step by step](/skills/a-plan-step-by-step/), [Cooperate or defend](/reference/strategy/cooperate-or-defend/), [Basics before brilliance](/skills/basics-before-brilliance/) and [Review calmly](/skills/review-calmly/). Each decision follows from the one before.

Chess players look again at every piece as the game changes, not only at the one that just moved. Off the board, giving what you already have a fresh look becomes a habit only if you practise it there, as this week's task asks. The lesson [Know when to stop](/skills/know-when-to-stop/) is about a different pull: what you have already put in.

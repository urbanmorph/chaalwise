    id: on-the-board
    text: On the chessboard, White moves first and keeps a small edge, called the initiative, which means White makes the threats and Black answers them. Black chooses how to answer. Black can play solidly to even the game out, then try to outplay White later. Or Black can make the game unbalanced, as the Sicilian Defence (1...c5) does here, and play for a win at more risk.
    position: 'moves: e4 c5'

  - kind: read
    id: off-the-board
    text: Off the board, someone always moves first, with the first offer or the first idea in a meeting. Moving first pays when you know what things are worth, and your move commits you or sets the starting point the other side must react to. Moving second pays when their move would tell you something you do not know. Ask which is true here. If you lead, keep the initiative, so they keep answering you. If you answer, choose on purpose. Either keep it level and solid, or change the shape of the problem.

Strong chess players move fast when the move is forced or obvious, and save their clock for the turning points. Off the board, asking how long a choice deserves becomes a habit only if you practise it there, as this week's task asks. The lesson [Spend effort where it matters](/skills/spend-effort-where-it-matters/) is about sharing out your time across many choices at once.

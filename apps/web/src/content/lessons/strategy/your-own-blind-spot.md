This lesson starts from the First number wins? lab: play it first, six estimates and then the same six again, in about five minutes. Carey Morewedge and colleagues (2015) trained this bias with a game in which players made the error, then had it named and got feedback on their own answers. It became less common, and the effect lasted two months or more.

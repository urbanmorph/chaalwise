Chess players learn to look at checks first, because the strongest move is often there, ahead of a pawn that looks free. Off the board, naming what a choice gives up becomes a habit only if you practise it there, as this week's task asks. The reference entry [Trade-offs](/reference/strategy/trade-offs/) has more on giving one thing for another.

At the poker table, players who bluff by asking "would I fold?" lose points to opponents who never fold. The habit costs more away from the table: in prices nobody pays, outings nobody enjoys and gifts nobody uses. Carey Morewedge and colleagues (2015) found that a game in which players made this error, then had it named and got feedback, made it less common for months afterwards.

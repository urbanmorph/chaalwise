Strong chess players stop calculating once every line they see leads to the same move. Off the board, asking what an answer would change becomes a habit only if you practise it there, as this week's task asks. The lesson [Estimate it](/skills/estimate-it/) helps when you do need a number, and [Look at three before choosing](/skills/look-at-three/) when you need more options rather than more facts.

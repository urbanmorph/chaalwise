    id: touch-move
    text: Over the board, meaning at a real board with a real opponent, the touch-move rule applies. If you touch one of your pieces, you must move it, if it has a legal move. If you touch an enemy piece, you must take it, if you can. To straighten a piece without moving it, say "I adjust" first. To castle, touch the king first and move it two squares, then move the rook with the same hand.

    id: clock-and-manners
    text: Most games use a clock. Each player has their own time, which runs only on their turn. Make your move, then press the clock with the same hand. If your time runs out, you lose. But if your opponent could not possibly mate you, it is a draw. To offer a draw, make your move, say "draw?", then press the clock. To resign, say "I resign" and offer your hand. In clubs you write every move on a score sheet, in the notation you learned. Shake hands before and after, and do not talk during play.

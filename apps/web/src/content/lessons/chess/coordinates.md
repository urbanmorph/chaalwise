---
title: Name the squares
summary: Every square has a name, file letter first, then rank number. Learn to find them without looking at the edges.
game: chess
stage: 0
order: 1
skills: []
steps:
  - kind: read
    id: files-and-ranks
    text: Files are the columns, lettered a to h from White's left. Ranks are the rows, numbered 1 to 8 from White's side. A square's name is its file then its rank, so e4 is on the e-file, fourth rank.
  - kind: square
    id: find-e4
    prompt: Find e4.
    answers: [e4]
    hint: The e-file is the fifth from the left. Count four rows up from your side.
    explanation: e4 is in the centre, on the king's file. It is the most common first move's destination.
  - kind: square
    id: find-d5
    prompt: Find d5.
    answers: [d5]
    hint: d is next to e, towards the a-file. Rank 5 is just over halfway up.
    explanation: d5 is one of the four centre squares, with d4, e4 and e5.
  - kind: square
    id: find-h1
    prompt: Find h1.
    answers: [h1]
    hint: The last file, on your own back rank.
    explanation: h1 is the corner square on your right when you sit behind the white pieces. The next lesson shows why that corner matters.
  - kind: square
    id: find-c6-black
    prompt: Now you are Black, looking from the other side. Find c6.
    orientation: black
    answers: [c6]
    hint: From Black's side the a-file is on your right, and rank 8 is nearest to you.
    explanation: From Black's side everything is turned round. c6 is three files from your right and three ranks up from your side.
---

You will read and say square names in every lesson from now on, so learn to find them fast. The board has no letters or numbers here: that is the point.

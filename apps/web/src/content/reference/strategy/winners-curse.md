Estimate what the thing is really worth. Suppose it is worth about the same to every bidder, and nobody knows exactly how much. Then bid a little below your estimate, because if you win, you were probably the most optimistic.

Sometimes the value really is personal, like a house you love for reasons no one else shares. Then winning carries no curse: just keep to the price you set.

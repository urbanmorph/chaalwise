The Prisoner's Dilemma, a classic game-theory puzzle, shows the tension. Each side does better for itself by defecting (breaking the deal), whatever the other does. Yet both do better if both cooperate.

Repetition is what keeps cooperation going. If you expect to meet again, and do not know when it will end, betrayal today costs you tomorrow.

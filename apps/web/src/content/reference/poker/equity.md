---
term: 'Equity'
game: 'poker'
kind: 'odds'
level: 'intermediate'
summary: 'Your share of the pot on average: the chance your hand ends up best, given the cards still to come.'
related: ['poker/outs', 'poker/expected-value', 'poker/range']
---

If your hand wins 60 times in 100 against the other hand, you have 60% equity. Equity says nothing about any single hand; it is what you would get on average if the situation were played many times.

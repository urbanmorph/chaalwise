---
term: 'Tilt'
game: 'poker'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'beginner'
aliases: ['On tilt']
summary: 'Playing worse because of frustration, often after a bad loss.'
related: ['strategy/cool-head', 'strategy/decisions-not-results']
---

Tilt turns one bad result into many bad decisions. The cure is the same as in chess: notice it, pause, and go back to your routine before the next decision.

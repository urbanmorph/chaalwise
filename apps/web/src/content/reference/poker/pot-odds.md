Say the pot holds 30 points and calling costs 10. You put in 10 to win a pot of 40 in total, so you need to win more than a quarter of the time (10 of the 40). Compare that with your chance of making your hand from your [outs](/reference/poker/outs/) (the unseen cards that would make it). If your chance is higher, the call pays over time.

A flush draw on the turn has 9 outs among 46 unseen cards, about a 20% chance. The call above needs 25%, so the draw should fold. If the call cost 5 into the same 30, the price would be 5 of 35, about 14%, and calling would pay.

Pot odds count only what is in the pot now. If you expect to win more in later betting when your card comes, see [implied odds](/reference/poker/implied-odds/).

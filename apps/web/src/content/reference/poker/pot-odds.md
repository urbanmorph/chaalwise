Say the pot holds 30 points and calling costs 10. You put in 10 to win a pot of 40 in total, so you need to win more than a quarter of the time (10 of the 40). Compare that with your chance of making your hand from your outs (the unseen cards that would make it). If your chance is higher, the call pays over time.

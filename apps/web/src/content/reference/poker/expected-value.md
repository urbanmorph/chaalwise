A call of 10 points that wins 40 points more a quarter of the time, and loses the 10 the rest of the time, has an expected value of 0.25 × 40 − 0.75 × 10 = 2.5 points: worth making, even though it loses most of the time.

This is the idea behind judging decisions rather than results.

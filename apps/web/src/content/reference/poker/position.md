faq:
  - q: 'What is position in poker?'
    a: 'Position is when you act in a betting round compared with the other players. After the first three shared cards, the player on the dealer button acts last in every round, so the button is the best seat.'
  - q: 'Why is acting last an advantage?'
    a: 'You see what everyone else did before you choose. If they all check, you can bet to take the pot, or check and see the next card for free. If someone bets, you know before you put any points in.'
  - q: 'Who acts first in poker?'
    a: 'Before the first three shared cards, the player to the left of the big blind acts first. After them, the first player still in the hand to the left of the button acts first, and the button acts last. With two players, the button posts the small blind and acts first until the shared cards come.'
  - q: 'Should you play more hands in late position?'
    a: 'Yes. Acting last makes every hand easier to play, so a hand too weak to play from an early seat can pay on the button. From an early seat, play fewer, stronger hands.'
---

Acting last means you see what everyone else did before you decide. That information is worth a lot, so players play more hands when they act late (late position) and fewer when they act early.

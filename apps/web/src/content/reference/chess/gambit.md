---
term: 'Gambit'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'intermediate'
summary: 'An opening that gives up material, usually a pawn, for time, development or attack.'
related: ['chess/kings-gambit', 'chess/queens-gambit', 'strategy/trade-offs']
---

A gambit is a trade-off: material now for activity now. It is sound if the activity can be turned into a real gain before the missing material starts to count.

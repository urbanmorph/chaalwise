Each move is a piece letter and a square. K is the king, Q the queen, R a rook, B a bishop and N a knight; pawns have no letter. So Nf3 is a knight to f3, and e4 is a pawn to e4. Here Black's next move, knight to c6, is written Nc6.

x marks a capture (Nxe5), + a check and # a checkmate. O-O is castling on the king's side, O-O-O on the queen's side. A promotion adds the new piece, as in e8=Q. A pawn capture names the file the pawn leaves, as in exd5. When two of the same piece can reach a square, the file or rank they start from is added, as in Nbd2 or R1e2.

Moves are numbered in pairs, White's then Black's: 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6. A Black move written on its own gets dots after its number, as in 2...Nc6.

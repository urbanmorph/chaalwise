term: 'Open file'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'intermediate'
summary: 'A file with no pawns on it, the natural road for rooks into the enemy position.'
related: ['chess/pawn-structure', 'chess/back-rank-mate']
lesson: 'chess/middlegame-intro'
---

Put a rook on an open file, then aim for the seventh rank, where the enemy pawns start. A half-open file, with only enemy pawns on it, is the next best thing.

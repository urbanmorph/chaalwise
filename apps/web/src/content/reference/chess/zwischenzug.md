game: 'chess'
kind: 'tactic'
level: 'intermediate'
aliases: ['In-between move', 'Intermezzo']
summary: 'Instead of the expected reply, an in-between move with a bigger threat comes first.'

lichess: 'intermezzo'
---

When you count a sequence of captures, check whether either side has a check or a bigger threat to play in the middle of it. An in-between move can turn a fair trade into a loss.

---
term: 'Double check'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'tactic'
level: 'intermediate'
summary: 'Two pieces give check at once, so the king must move: nothing can block or capture both.'
related: ['chess/discovered-attack', 'chess/smothered-mate']
---

A double check comes from a discovered check where the moving piece also checks. Blocking or capturing deals with only one checker, so the king itself has to move. If it cannot, it is mate.

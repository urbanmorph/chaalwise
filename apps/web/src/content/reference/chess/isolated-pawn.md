---
term: 'Isolated pawn'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'intermediate'
aliases: ['Isolani', 'IQP']
summary: 'A pawn with no pawns of its own side on the files next to it, so no pawn can defend it.'
related: ['chess/pawn-structure', 'chess/passed-pawn']
---

An isolated pawn is a weakness in the endgame, because pieces must defend it. In the middlegame it can be a strength, giving open lines and outposts for an attack.

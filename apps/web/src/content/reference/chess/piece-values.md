The values guide trades: giving a rook for a knight loses about two pawns of value. They are averages. Most players rate a bishop slightly above a knight, and having both bishops as worth about half a pawn extra. A well-placed knight can be worth more than a bishop blocked in by pawns. The king has no value, because it can never be traded.

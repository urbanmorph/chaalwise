---
term: 'Outpost'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'intermediate'
summary: 'A square in enemy territory that no enemy pawn can attack, ideal for a knight.'
related: ['chess/pawn-structure', 'chess/isolated-pawn']
---

A knight on an outpost, supported by a pawn, can be as strong as a rook, because only a piece can ever challenge it.

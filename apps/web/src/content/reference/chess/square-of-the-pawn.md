---
term: 'Square of the pawn'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'endgame'
level: 'beginner'
aliases: ['Rule of the square']
summary: 'A quick test of whether a king can catch a passed pawn, without counting moves.'
related: ['chess/passed-pawn', 'chess/opposition']
lesson: 'chess/endgame-intro'
---

Draw a square from the pawn to its queening square, with that as one side. If the defending king can step into the square, it catches the pawn. If not, the pawn queens, unless other pieces help.

term: 'Square of the pawn'

summary: 'A quick test of whether a king can catch a passed pawn, without counting moves.'

Picture a square on the board. One side of it runs from the pawn to its queening square, where it would promote. If the defending king can step into the square, it catches the pawn. If not, the pawn queens, unless other pieces help.

Two adjustments make it right. If it is the pawn's side to move, push the pawn one square in your head first. A pawn still on its starting rank can move two squares, so draw the square as if it stood one rank further up.

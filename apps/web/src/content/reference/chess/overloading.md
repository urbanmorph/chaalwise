---
term: 'Overloading'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'tactic'
level: 'intermediate'
aliases: ['Overworked piece']
summary: 'One piece has to defend two things at once, and cannot.'
related: ['chess/removing-the-defender', 'chess/fork']
---

Attack the first thing the piece defends. When it captures or moves to defend it, the second thing is left unguarded.

---
term: 'Castling'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'rule'
level: 'beginner'
aliases: ['O-O', 'O-O-O', 'Castle']
summary: 'The king moves two squares towards a rook, and the rook jumps over to the other side, in one move.'
related: ['chess/king-safety']
lesson: 'chess/castling'
---

Allowed only if neither piece has moved, the squares between them are empty, the king is not in check, and it does not pass through or land on an attacked square. Short castling is written O-O, long castling O-O-O.

You may castle only if neither piece has moved and the squares between them are empty. The king must not be in check, and must not pass through or land on an attacked square. Only the king's squares matter; the rook may cross an attacked square, such as b1 when castling long. Short castling is written O-O, long castling O-O-O.

Castling counts as a king move. Over the board, touch the king first; online, drag the king two squares.

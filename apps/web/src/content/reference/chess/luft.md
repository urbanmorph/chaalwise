Usually it is h3 (or ...h6 for Black). The moves f3 or f4 also give the king a square, but they open the diagonal from a7 to g1 towards it. And g3 leaves weak squares on f3 and h3. Make it when you have a spare moment, before the back rank becomes a problem, not after.

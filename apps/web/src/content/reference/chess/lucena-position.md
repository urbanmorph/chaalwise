term: 'Lucena position'

summary: 'The key winning position in rook endings: rook, king and pawn on the seventh against a rook, with the defending king cut off.'
related: ['chess/philidor-position', 'chess/passed-pawn']
---

The stronger side's king stands on the queening square in front of its pawn, and the defending king is cut off by at least one file. Without that cut-off it is often a draw. The stronger side shelters its king from checks by using its own rook as a bridge, then queens the pawn. Knowing it, and the Philidor position, covers most rook and pawn against rook endings.

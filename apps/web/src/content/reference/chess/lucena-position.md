---
term: 'Lucena position'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'endgame'
level: 'advanced'
aliases: ['Building a bridge']
summary: 'The key winning position in rook endings: king and pawn on the seventh against a rook.'
related: ['chess/philidor-position', 'chess/passed-pawn']
---

The stronger side shelters its king from checks by using its own rook as a bridge, then queens the pawn. Knowing it, and the Philidor position, covers most rook and pawn against rook endings.

term: 'Lucena position'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'endgame'
level: 'advanced'
aliases: ['Building a bridge']
summary: 'The key winning position in rook endings: king, rook and a pawn on the seventh rank against a rook, with the defending king cut off.'
related: ['chess/philidor-position', 'chess/passed-pawn']
---

The stronger side's king stands on the queening square, in front of its own pawn. The defending king is cut off from the pawn by at least one file. Without that cut-off it is often a draw. The stronger side shelters its king from checks by using its own rook as a bridge, then queens the pawn. Knowing it, and the Philidor position, covers most rook and pawn against rook endings.

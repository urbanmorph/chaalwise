game: 'chess'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'intermediate'
aliases: ['Tempi', 'Gaining time']
summary: 'A unit of time: one move. Gaining a tempo means making progress while forcing the other side to respond.'
related: ['strategy/lead-or-answer', 'chess/development', 'chess/gambit']
---

Developing a piece with a threat gains a tempo: the other side spends its move answering. In the opening, a few tempi can decide who controls the game.

---
term: 'Zugzwang'
game: 'chess'
kind: 'endgame'
level: 'intermediate'
aliases: ['Compulsion to move']
summary: 'Any move makes your position worse, but you must move.'
related: ['chess/opposition', 'chess/triangulation', 'chess/stalemate']
---

Usually having the move is an advantage. In zugzwang it is a burden: every legal move gives something away. It is rare in the middlegame and common in endgames, especially king and pawn endings.

It is not stalemate: in zugzwang there are legal moves, they are just all bad. Opposition and triangulation are ways to put the other side in zugzwang.

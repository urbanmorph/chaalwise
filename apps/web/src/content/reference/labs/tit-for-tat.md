---
term: 'Tit for tat'
game: 'labs'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'beginner'
summary: 'Cooperate first, then copy what the other player did last time.'
related: ['labs/prisoners-dilemma', 'strategy/cooperate-or-defend']
---

Simple, and famously successful in repeated Prisoner's Dilemma tournaments: it is nice (never betrays first), retaliates, forgives, and is easy for others to understand. A slightly forgiving version does even better when mistakes happen.

Tit for tat never beats the player it faces: at best it ties. It does best overall because it gets others to cooperate. It is not unbeatable either; when moves can be misread, other simple rules can do better.

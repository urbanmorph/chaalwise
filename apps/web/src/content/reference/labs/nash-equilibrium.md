---
term: 'Nash equilibrium'
game: 'labs'
kind: 'concept'
level: 'intermediate'
summary: 'A set of choices where no player can do better by changing only their own.'
related: ['labs/prisoners-dilemma', 'labs/mixed-strategy', 'labs/zero-sum-game']
---

Each player's choice is a best answer to the others'. An equilibrium need not be good for anyone. In the Prisoner's Dilemma, two players each choose to cooperate or betray. Both betraying is the equilibrium, yet both would prefer that both cooperate.

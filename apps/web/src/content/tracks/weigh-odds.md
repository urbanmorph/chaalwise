idea: 'Put numbers on uncertainty instead of going on gut feeling.'
why: "Rough numbers beat vague feelings. Saying 'about one in four' lets you compare options, notice when a cheap risk is worth taking, and see when a price is too high for your chances."
atWork: 'Before a launch, estimate how likely each outcome is and what each is worth, instead of planning only for the one you hope for.'
inStudy: 'When choosing courses or applications, estimate your real chances for each and spread your effort to match.'
habit: 'Roughly how likely is this, and what is it worth if it happens?'

---
idea: 'Commit in proportion to your edge, so that one bad outcome cannot ruin you.'
why: 'Even good bets lose sometimes. Whoever stakes everything on one good chance is eventually wiped out by bad luck; whoever stakes a sensible share stays in the game long enough for the edge to pay.'
atWork: 'Pilot the new product with one region or one customer group before you move the whole budget to it.'
inStudy: 'Do not stake the whole grade on one ambitious project; keep the core work safe and take the risk where a miss costs little.'
habit: 'If this goes wrong, can I recover, and is the stake worth the edge?'

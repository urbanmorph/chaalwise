---
idea: 'Stop being predictable where someone can take advantage of it.'
why: 'Patterns are useful until someone working against you reads them. A player who always does the same thing is easy to beat; mixing your choices, where an opponent is watching, keeps them guessing. Where people rely on you, being predictable is a strength.'
atWork: 'Vary when and how you run spot checks, so that the process is followed every day, not just on the day everyone knows about.'
inStudy: 'In a debate or a moot, vary how you open your case, so that a rival who has watched you before cannot prepare for it.'
habit: 'Could someone watching me guess what I will do next, and would it cost me?'

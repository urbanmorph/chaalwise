---
idea: 'Price things fairly, and avoid overpaying when you win.'
why: 'When a prize is worth about the same to every bidder and nobody knows exactly how much, winning often means you valued it more than anyone else, which can mean you overvalued it. Knowing what it is worth, and allowing for that, before the bidding starts keeps the win a win.'
atWork: 'Before the negotiation, set your walk-away price for a hire, a contract or buying a business, the most you will pay. Then keep to it.'
inStudy: 'Before taking on an extra activity, work out what it is worth to you in hours, and what it will push out.'
habit: 'What is this worth to me, decided before I start bidding?'
